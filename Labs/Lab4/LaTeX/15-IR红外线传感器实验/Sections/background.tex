\section{Theory and Technical Background}
The TCRT5000~\ref{fig:TCRT5000} is a small photoelectric component that belongs to the category of infrared invisible light products. 
It is capable of detecting changes in light intensity. 
The infrared-emitting diode of this device continuously emits infrared light. When the emitted infrared light is not reflected or the reflected intensity is insufficient, the phototransistor remains in the cut-off state. 
The module's output terminal is at a low voltage level, and the indicator diode stays off. 
When an object to be detected enters the detection range, the infrared light is reflected back with sufficient intensity. 
The phototransistor then enters the saturation state, the module's output terminal switches to a high voltage level, and the indicator diode is illuminated.
