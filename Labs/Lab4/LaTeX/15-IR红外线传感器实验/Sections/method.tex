\textbf{Key words: IDE, IR, Variable Control}

\subsection{Experiment 1}
We measured the changes in the ADC values of the IR sensor at intervals of every 0.5 cm from a white sheet of paper, obtaining a total of 10 sets of data ranging from 0.5 cm to 5 cm.

The program is written using Arduino IDE to obtain the voltage value from analog pin A0 and convert it to a digital value through ADC. 
The corresponding sensorValue is obtained through the Serial Monitor in the IDE.

\subsection{Experiment 2}
In this experiment, the sensors were installed at a height of 80 millimeters above the chassis. 
The program is written using Arduino IDE to obtain the voltage value from analog pin A0 and convert it to a digital value through ADC. 
The corresponding sensorValue is obtained through the Serial Monitor in the IDE. 
By reading the sensor values, we can accurately determine whether each sensor is positioned over a white surface or a black line.
