\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}
\subsection*{Conclusion and Summary}
In this experiment, we accomplished the following tasks:
\begin{enumerate}
	\item By conducting multiple measurements of the height between the infrared sensor and the ground and the corresponding changes in readings, we ensured the accuracy of the readings and the reliability of the data, and ultimately plotted a variation curve. 
	With the help of this curve, we were able to determine the optimal height range for installing the infrared sensor, which provided a scientific basis for the sensor installation position in subsequent experiments and laid a solid foundation.
	\item By analyzing the differences in readings of the infrared sensor on the white ground and the black line, we established the identification criteria for different reading ranges corresponding to the ground and the line to be followed. 
	This achievement provided strong technical support for the subsequent line-following tasks, ensuring the accuracy and reliability of the system.
\end{enumerate}

\subsection*{Limitations of the experiment}
\begin{enumerate}
	\item Due to the limitations of our experimental conditions, we could only measure the distance between the sensor and the ground manually, which may have caused some deviations in the sensor readings.
	\item Our experiment only considered the scenario where the ground is white under the same lighting conditions. 
	This might lead to interference for the robot car when encountering different colored surfaces during the actual line-following process.
\end{enumerate}
