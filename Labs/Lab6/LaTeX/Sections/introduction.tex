\section{Introduction}
\label{sec:introduction}

\textbf{Experiment: Measurement of the maximum distance that can be measured to an obstacle at different angles}

This laboratory aims on discovering the maximum distance that can be measured in a series of  particular angles.
A radar chart of all accessible data is produced to offer a visible approach.
We measured the maximum distance of detectable obstacles at different angles within
a range of 0 to 400 centimeters from the obstacle and plotted the data into a
radar chart to intuitively display the detection range in various directions.
We find that there are much more figure from 80 to 100 degrees.
