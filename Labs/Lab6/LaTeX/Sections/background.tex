\section{Theory and Technical Background}
\label{sec:theory-and-technical-background}
To achieve ultrasonic distance measurement, the main components we use are
the \textbf{HC-SR04} ultrasonic distance measurement module and
the \textbf{SG90} servo motor.

\subsection{HC-SR04 ultrasonic distance measurement module}

The \textbf{HC-SR04} (Figure~\ref{fig:hc-sr04}) ultrasonic distance measurement module
consists of an ultrasonic transmitter and a receiver.
There are two important pins.
\textbf{Trig pin}: In fact, it is trigger, which means to trigger or cause.
At that time, only a high level of 10 μ s needs to be applied to this pin, and
HC-SR04 will automatically emit 8 square waves of 40KHz (that is, ultrasonic waves).
\textbf{Echo pin}: echo actually means echo.
When the HC-SR04 successfully emits ultrasonic waves outward,
this pin will become a high level,
and the high level will continue until the HC-SR04 receives the echo.

\begin{figure}[H]
  \centering
  \includegraphics[width=0.5\linewidth]{Figures/HC-SR04}
  \rule{35em}{0.5pt}
  \caption{HC-SR04 ultrasonic distance measurement module}
  \label{fig:hc-sr04}
\end{figure}

The transmitter sends ultrasonic waves in a specific direction.
\begin{itemize}
  \item When the ultrasonic waves encounter an obstacle, they are reflected back, and
    the receiver captures the reflected waves.
    By calculating the time difference between the emission and reception of the ultrasonic waves,
    combined with the speed of sound, the distance can be determined.
  \item When the ultrasonic wave is emitted, the Echo pin becomes high.
  \item When the ultrasonic wave is received by the receiver, the Echo pin becomes low.
\end{itemize}
By measuring the duration of the high state of the Echo pin,
the time of ultrasonic wave propagation can be obtained.

\subsection{SG90 servo motor}

The SG90 (Figure~\ref{fig:sg90}) servo motor is a position (angle) servo driver,
suitable for control systems that
require continuous angle changes and the ability to maintain a specific angle.
It is mainly composed of a casing, circuit board, DC motor, gear reduction set,
and position detection element (control circuit), forming an automatic (closed-loop) control device.

The data cable of the servo should be connected to the \textbf{PWM} pin, thus,
the PWM value can be correctly passed in.
