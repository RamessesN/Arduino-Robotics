\section{Conclusions}
\label{sec:conclusions}
\textbf{Conclusion and Summary}:

In this experiment, we accomplished the following tasks:

1）We collected three sets of data at angles ranging from 0 to 180 degrees and
distances ranging from 0 to 400cm from the obstacle, and then calculated the
average of these three sets of data.

2）Based
on the calculated averages, we created two radar charts.
One illustrates the maximum distance at which obstacles can be detected at each angle, while
the other shows how far obstacles can be detected at different angles when
the forward linear distance is the same.

\textbf{Limitations of the experiment}:

1）
We cannot rule out all environmental influences, as the ultrasonic waves may
have reflected off objects other than the intended obstacles.

2）
After obtaining the experimental data, we manually removed outliers, which may
have led to the inadvertent deletion of some valid values.
