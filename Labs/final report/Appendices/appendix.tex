\chapter{Contribution Statement}
\label{ch:contribution-statement}

The development of \gls{car} represents a collaborative effort with distinct yet complementary contributions from all team members. \textbf{Yuwei Zhao} spearheaded the experimental implementation and coding development while overseeing the physical construction process, including component integration and wiring optimization. As the primary architect of the lobster-inspired design, \textbf{Haohao Qi} created the detailed 3D models using Blender software and managed the 3D printing workflow, additionally documenting key dimensional measurements throughout the fabrication process. \textbf{Yilin Zhang} contributed significantly to the experimental design phase, particularly in developing control logic architectures, while taking primary responsibility for technical documentation and report composition.

\textbf{All members} participated in the final bodywork assembly phase following the 3D printing completion, with Yuwei and Yilin assisting in sensor mounting and aesthetic finishing. This collective approach extended to data collection activities, where team members rotated measurement duties during experimental validation sessions to ensure consistent observation standards. The modular \gls{pcb} implementations benefited from cross-verification, with each member validating at least two circuit designs beyond their primary assignments.
